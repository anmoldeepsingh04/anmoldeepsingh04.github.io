Hi, I hope you're doing well!

I am Anmoldeep Singh, currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Computational Science and Engineering at the Technical University of Munich. I earned my Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from the <a href='https://iitgoa.ac.in/'> Indian Institute of Technology, Goa</a> in 2022.

My academic interests lie at the intersection of scientific computing, numerical simulation, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), machine learning, and high-performance computing. I am particularly interested in combining mathematical modeling and data-driven methods to better understand and solve complex engineering and scientific problems.

Before beginning my Master’s studies, I worked as an Innovation Engineer at Thermax Limited, where I worked on projects in the alternate and renewable energy space. My work included developing data-driven control systems for biomass-fired boilers and contributing to the design of biogas and green hydrogen purification systems. These experiences strengthened my interest in computational methods for engineering design and practical problem-solving.

I am now exploring the integration of computational modeling, applied mathematics, and machine learning, with a particular interest in physics-informed and scientific machine learning. My long-term goal is to develop computational tools that bridge fundamental scientific understanding with real-world engineering applications.
